---
title: Glidepath Privacy Policy
---


_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

Glidepath is designed so your health information never leaves your device.

## What we store
Everything you enter — medications, doses, injection sites, weights, protein, water, side effects and notes — is stored **only on your device**. We do not operate servers that receive this data, and we have no way to see it.

## Accounts
Glidepath has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. A first name, if you choose to enter one, stays on your device.

## Purchases
Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm whether you have an active Pro purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts — never your health data. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

## Analytics and advertising
Glidepath contains no ads, no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics.

## Sharing
Your data is only shared when **you** choose to export a CSV or PDF report, using your device's share sheet.

## Deleting your data
Use **More → Delete all data**, or uninstall the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children
Glidepath is not intended for anyone under 18.
